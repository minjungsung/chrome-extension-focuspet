# FocusPet privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-10-16.

## Data and access

Local data includes timer state, durations, task intention, companion type and name, total completed focus sessions, and up to 90 days of daily counts. No page content or browsing history is accessed.

`storage` persists timer state and companion progress. `alarms` schedules timer completion. `notifications` displays generic completion messages.

Processing is local. The developer receives no extension data. No accounts, cloud sync, ads, analytics or remote executable code. Google and Chrome have separate privacy policies.

## Control and retention

Saved data remains until deleted, extension storage is cleared, or the extension is uninstalled. Exported files and clipboard contents remain under your control and may contain private content. FocusPet keeps up to 90 days of daily counts and a lifetime companion total.

## Contact

Use this repository’s issue tracker or the store support contact. Never post private information publicly.
