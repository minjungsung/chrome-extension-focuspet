# FocusPet launch material

## Community post

Title: A focus timer. A little companion.

I built FocusPet, a Chrome extension for a simpler everyday browsing workflow.

FocusPet is a focus timer with a tiny virtual cat or bunny to keep you company. Start a 5, 15, or 25-minute session, finish one small task, and earn accessories for your companion.

What it does:
- Choose 5, 15, or 25 minutes
- Completed sessions unlock accessories
- Pause, resume, and take a short break

Install: https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/cbfjjbjnoogmgpggefjgphhdfdpciclk

I am the developer. I would appreciate feedback on the workflow, confusing steps, or missing essentials. Please share your own experience; there is no reward or required rating.
